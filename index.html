Our Story

The Rooftop Awaits

Perched atop B Square in the heart of Rewa, THE HIGH TRIBE is more than a restaurant — it's a destination. A rooftop sanctuary where the city skyline meets the stars, and every evening becomes a celebration.

From premium dining under the open sky to curated nightlife experiences, we blend culinary artistry with an atmosphere of refined luxury. Whether it's an intimate dinner, a lively gathering, or a private event, our rooftop sets the stage for unforgettable moments.
